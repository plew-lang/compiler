# Evaluate the revised workflow on one bounded lexical binding repair

## Scope and Boundary

Evaluate the method on one real bounded lexical-binding/visibility repair
selected by com-x7r3. That task owns code, tests, old-path removal and semantic
review. This child owns process observation and effectiveness, not a duplicated
implementation or separate semantic admission. Its exact repair dependency must
be installed before this trial begins; waiting for the whole phase is unnecessary.

## Actions and Exit

Observe the repair from its normal task entry: contract and scope, independently
grounded regression, intended RED on the fixed old candidate, responsibility-level
repair through final use, GREEN and actual independent review. Record what was
really performed, whether the old bug is detected, whether competing paths are
removed and what remains outside scope. Pure refactors use the documented exception.

Check a legal additional contrast not chosen solely from the implementation story.
Assess missing steps, repeated same-class failures, resumption/search effort,
user interruptions and observable time/token/performance costs; do not invent
unavailable metrics. Current formal performance/gate owners remain unchanged.

Propose concrete simplifications when process adds no evidence. A repeated
recording omission can justify a small future automation proposal, not automatic
introduction of a guard/CI platform. Obtain an independent bounded review of the
trial evidence. Close only after the selected actual repair and review are complete;
this does not certify all remaining phase-three contracts.
