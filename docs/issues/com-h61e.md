## Contract and Scope

intake under com-ax5g. Own phase-four admission of prepared-execution sessions,
finalized metadata/layout/physical services, LLVM/native emission and observation
boundaries. Design: compiler-state.md, llvm-backend.md, representation-and-abi.md,
driver-and-execution.md and diagnostics.md. Existing source/Mid facts are inputs;
these consumers must not gain unrelated mutable compiler/source-publication

## Execution and Admission

This is an area epic, not one executable repair or a new backend architecture.
Actual prerequisite repairs own their exact API/delegate/caller/control scopes,
producer-to-final use, removed broad routes and independent TDD/refactor evidence.
Only grounded adopted intake expands these dependencies. Cross-domain owners
retain all scoped roles; one primary assignment never drops other consumers.

Close after every required assigned repair and scoped area review. Verify same
session/body/pool/native owner lifetimes, concrete/physical derived writers,
error/default/restore/order and AOT/JIT consumers. Preserve coherent LLVM
ownership and legitimate caches/interner; no full Comp hidden behind a facade.
Required whole-candidate gates/performance/main admission stay with com-604k.
Do not claim product repair from intake closure or waive open required work.

## Excluded

New target/host API, workers/reusable Restart, language/ABI/runtime semantic
changes, new services merely for their own sake and optional passive-value
simplifications. Existing source-only audit evidence is not current product
GREEN or a whole-backend algorithm proof.
