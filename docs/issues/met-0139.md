## Observed Asymmetry and Unverified Consequence

The [driver input contract](../design/driver-and-execution.md#fixed-inputs-and-session-authority)
and spec15 require equivalent admitted inputs and common semantics for equivalent
requests. Different body-checking scopes are intentional.

[Distribution](../../distribution/_.pw)::main routes `check` directly to runCheck;
[Tool](../../native/tool.cpp) calls resolveIfNeeded for build/run after mode
selection, while gen follows its own build/harness path.
[Resolver](../../resolve/Resolver.pw)::needsResolution currently treats existence
of Plew.lock as sufficient to skip automatic resolution. These are confirmed
structural differences, not yet a reproduced wrong check result or proof that
check is required to perform exactly the same external commands.

work-scope differences, then repair only real contract violations without
