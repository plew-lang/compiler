# Retain generic call substitution conflict ownership

## Contract and scope

A generic call-binding Conflict must keep its original decision through the
owning source consumer. Cover independently typed and prepared contextual
arguments, both selected and diagnostic-candidate adapter roles, known bindings,
parameter mapping, free/member/associated/view/named-factory operations and
function/global/closure owners. Preserve rejection wording/location and valid
call selection; do not manufacture a successful parent result or template.

Public Raw inference, including Mono, retains ordinary unowned diagnostics even
with an active cursor. Dependency errors pass through unchanged. Equal text,
a cursor, copied spans or a synthetic call are not source ownership. Opaque
numeric expectations belong to met-9ym8; whole-suite reconciliation belongs to
com-yeua. This finite task cannot close either parent or promote main/seed.

## Responsibility boundary

- The existing typed-term and prepared matchers decide Conflict once.
  Transactional extension commits only Matched/Deferred results. Failure keeps
  the known pre-match map, not a partially mutated candidate.
- A narrow private inference origin carries the general source request,
  authored operation, known callee and role. It does not carry expected-result
  or Array-frontier authority. Private rejection events retain location,
  operand/parameter mapping, callee declaration, binder identities and known
  values. The common owner observer validates those identities without matching
  types again or publishing success.
- Ranking normalizes lexical Self and uses the candidate receiver recipe plus
  the existing tentative matcher. It must not invoke strict selected-call
  inference first. A conflicting candidate cannot prevent another valid
  candidate from being considered. Strict inference stays at the selected or
  diagnostic source call, while Raw wrappers remain Raw.
- General request identity is separate from Array-specific Regular,
  Diagnostic(frontier) and Unavailable admission. Returning/recursive scope
  changes preserve both independently. Regular alone grants nothing; valid
  request, owner and expected contract remain necessary. Unavailable and Raw
  cannot issue owned Array failure evidence.
- A passive normalized factory operation is established from its original
  construction, private site serial and arguments, including an uninstantiated
  qualifier. A derived semantic receiver is not needed for Generic authority.
  Array admission separately requires a valid nonzero factory receiver.

These boundaries follow diagnostics.md, body-checking.md and shared callable
applicability in name-lookup.md. Independent source review accepted the separation
and verified the Raw/public call graph. Earlier review findings about member
role and callee/label revalidation are implemented.

## Required scenario reconciliation

1. Typed and prepared conflicts; known/independent/rigid bindings; omitted-default
   argument mapping; both callee roles. Preserve actual DiagnosticCandidate lookup;
   test a supplied Selected adapter contract separately without claiming that
   inconsistent arguments naturally ranked Selected.
2. Raw and active-owner calls; foreign origin and refusal session/root/callee/
   operand/parameter/event/location; changed arguments and synthetic operations;
   equal-text Internal reports; private binder/declaration snapshots.
3. Raw cache/drain, repeated owning lazy requests, whole and eager consumers;
   nested failure retains its inner call; no parent result, template, closure or
   callable-interface publication after rejection.
4. Array Regular/Diagnostic/Unavailable, Raw and foreign inputs, frontier bounds,
   recursive/returning preservation; Generic ownership survives unavailable
   Array authority. Existing Array consumers and mutation controls remain valid.
5. Bare/explicit receivers, ordinary and prepared generic/projection controls,
   receiver-pattern precedence and numeric frontiers. The alternative valid
   factory must execute its own body (golden 2), not merely compile.
6. Exact final source/candidate, affected public/native/gen gates, LLVM22
   ASan/LSan, formal fixed point and median at most 7.5 seconds, and independent
   final source/evidence acceptance.
