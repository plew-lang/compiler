# Review phase-one conformance completion independently

## Scope and Method

Independent review of phase-one completion, not implementation conformance or compiler performance. Reviewed input: compiler `cf2cc2ef29cc67798737aa4aa4b3e195ceeb4184`, meta `c5cb1ef3`, spec `1e0c4d08777edc521e0e64f42588d4996b1c66d8` and Syntax `00f740e1178e5b992cfdd30ddaa5a0612ce27b43`. Reviewers also inspected the subsequent documentation-only fixes recorded below.

Three independent agents checked complementary questions:

- `phase1_scope_review`: whether the 53 original open issues and audited domains distinguish current contracts from new feature families without weakening adopted design.
- `phase1_units_review`: whether bounded units name producers, publication, final consumers, removal, controls and actual prerequisites, and can be completed independently.
- `phase1_completion_review`: whether the completion claim, dependency closure, progress plan, worklog and preserved evidence support starting the selected Buffer increment.

The review checked the issue contents and dependency graph, not just issue counts or the presence of headings. Bounded verification tasks may retain implementation unknowns; an unresolved current-feature versus new-feature classification may not be deferred that way.

## Findings and Resolution

**P2 — New macro public forms could enter the current-feature scope.** The included com-vy62 scope did not clearly separate current gen handoff/framing/receipt work from the absent `macro`/`macrointerface` declarations and role registration/publication. Syntax `src/_.pw` still declares ordinary Derive/ParameterizedDerive traits, and compiler `src/Codegen/Gen.pw` uses those trait providers and argument-count form selection. The adopted public-role contract is a separate absent feature, not a necessary premise of all current gen repairs.

Resolved by [met-8b8w](met-8b8w.md): three end-to-end increments cover current provider forms, library-owned String handoffs, host isolation/framing and recorded-input receipt admission. [com-vy62](com-vy62.md#existing-feature-conformance-classification) keeps the new public-role family. The integration epic depends on the child instead of the mixed parent. Neither the child nor its receipt certifies future role facts, adopts the legacy provider model as the final design, or closes that parent. The scope reviewer checked that existing trait-provided methods and internal execution/artifact boundaries can realize the child without requiring the new declarations.

**P3 — Startup summary producer ownership needed clarification.** [com-fkxl](com-fkxl.md) now explicitly owns generating, retaining and closing its startup summaries using the common Mid/AmbientEffects machinery. Access-summary success alone does not prove startup readiness. No unspecified producer or additional feature dependency remains implied.

The classification record, cross-domain mapping, integration dependencies and meta progress/worklog are synchronized to ten scoped work units. Original classifications remain a pinned scope record rather than a second live status index.

## Re-review and Outcome

All three agents inspected the fixes and accepted phase-one completion. The scope reviewer found P2 resolved without a new specification decision; the units reviewer found P3 resolved and the tenth unit executable; the completion reviewer confirmed the new child is reachable, the future macro parent is not required by the integration dependency closure, and no required current contract was lost.

**Phase one is complete: scope classification and bounded work-unit planning are sufficient to start implementation.** This does not certify existing-feature implementation conformance or close any repair task. No compiler code, specification, seed, carrier, test or performance result was changed or generated by this review.
